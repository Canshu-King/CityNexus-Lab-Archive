==================================================
8. PREEMPTIVE AUTOPILOT
==================================================

V5 reacts after disruption.

V6 may act before hard failure.

Example:

Forecast says heavy rain will overlap Riverside Walk in 45 minutes.
The cruise has only 18 minutes of travel buffer after the walk.
Counterfactual simulation shows a high probability of Journey infeasibility.

The Agent may preemptively replace/reschedule the ordinary Activity if:
- the standing mission permits it;
- expected risk reduction is meaningful;
- switching cost is small;
- the action is reversible;
- the Autonomy Envelope permits it;
- protected commitments remain safe;
- deterministic post-action feasibility passes.

Persist why the action happened before the actual failure.
