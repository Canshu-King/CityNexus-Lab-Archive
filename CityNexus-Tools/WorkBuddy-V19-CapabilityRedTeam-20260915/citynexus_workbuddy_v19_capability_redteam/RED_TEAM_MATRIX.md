1. wheelchair traveler
2. elderly parent with walking limit
3. infant family with nap
4. stroller family
5. large-luggage traveler
6. traveler with toilet/rest frequency
7. sensory/quiet need
8. normal traveler, no special need
9. conflicting family needs
10. ambiguous statement
11. negated/historical need
12. malicious prompt trying to bypass hard constraints

Tianjin, Fuzhou, Beijing, Shanghai, Chongqing, Tokyo, Singapore, London,
and at least three smaller destinations.

- hard need downgraded to preference
- unknown evidence treated as verified
- false provider accessibility
- replan loses capability
- C4 protection vs capability conflict
- semantic false positive
- semantic false negative
- stale capability scope
- cross-Journey leakage
- learned-soft becomes hard
