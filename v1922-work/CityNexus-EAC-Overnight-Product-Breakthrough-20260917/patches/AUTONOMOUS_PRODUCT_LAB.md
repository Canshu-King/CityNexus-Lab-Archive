# AUTONOMOUS PRODUCT LAB

If required work finishes before the overnight window is substantially used,
continue autonomously.

Do not invent random architecture.

Use the application.

Ask repeatedly:

- Would a first-time traveler know what to do next?
- Is the most important action visible without absurd scrolling?
- Does any page expose implementation vocabulary?
- Does selecting one thing unexpectedly destroy another?
- Can the user recover after network/AI/GPS failure?
- Is there a route that looks complete but is actually only a placeholder?
- Are unknown facts being presented as known?
- Are known facts being hidden as unknown?
- Are there visual dead ends or overloaded screens?
- Can a traveler complete the flow one-handed on a phone?

Choose the highest-impact discovered problem, fix it, add evidence, and rerun the
relevant traveler flow.

Continue until:
- the requested 8–9 hour run is substantially complete; or
- every remaining blocker depends on an external system you cannot access.

Do not stop simply because you ran out of assigned bullet points.
